## FastH3

FastH3 generates video and audio from text. V2 is the eight-step release;
Trim is the smaller experimental variant. Choose the export used by your
runtime's setup guide.

## FastMetal

FastMetal packages distilled Wan models with INT8 quantization-aware-trained
DiTs for Apple Silicon. The family has 1.3B, 5B, and 14B checkpoints.

| Model              | Checkpoint on Hugging Face                                        |

| FastMetal 1.3B QAD | [View model](https://huggingface.co/FastVideo/FastMetal-1.3B-QAD) |
